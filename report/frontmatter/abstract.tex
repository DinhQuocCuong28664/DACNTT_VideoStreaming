\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

A video sharing platform must turn every upload, in any format, codec or resolution, into HTTP
Live Streaming (HLS) renditions that a browser can play at adaptive quality. Transcoding is a bursty
workload: a server cluster kept running to wait for uploads pays for idle capacity most of the time,
yet the platform must still absorb many uploads arriving at once.

This project designs, builds and evaluates a complete video sharing platform whose transcoding runs
on serverless containers driven by events. An upload to Amazon S3 emits an event that Amazon SQS
buffers and an AWS Lambda function submits to AWS Batch on AWS Fargate, where a container packaging
FFmpeg exists only while it works: it produces up to six renditions from 144p to 1080p, a master
playlist and a thumbnail, and then terminates. Videos of five minutes or more are split into chunks
encoded in parallel. Amazon CloudFront delivers the streams, and Origin Access Control together with
CloudFront Signed Cookies keeps a private video unreachable even to someone who knows its manifest
URL. Uploads are screened with Amazon Rekognition, and staff review doubtful videos in a console
whose recorded decisions make the error rates of the screening measurable. The infrastructure is
described in thirteen Terraform modules, and seven GitHub Actions workflows run the tests, the
security scans and the deployment.

Measured on the deployed system, a four-hour video was ready 135 minutes after its upload at the
production quota of 8~vCPU, and one hundred clips submitted at once were all processed without a
failure. Measuring the time to first frame exposed a content delivery configuration that served
Vietnam from Europe; correcting it cut the median from 727.69~ms to 110.32~ms. For the part of the
bill that the architecture controls, the estimated monthly cost is 3.40~USD against 133.35~USD for
an always-on instance. The saving comes from removing idle capacity, and it narrows as sustained
utilisation rises.
